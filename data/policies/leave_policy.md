# Leave Policy

Nimbus Technologies Pvt. Ltd. — Policy HR-01 — Effective 1 April 2026

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy explains the types of leave available to employees of Nimbus Technologies, how much leave each employee is entitled to, and how to apply for it. It applies to all full-time permanent employees. Interns and contract staff follow the terms written in their individual contracts.

## 2. Leave Entitlements

Every full-time employee receives the following leave each calendar year (January to December):

- Annual (earned) leave: 18 working days
- Sick leave: 10 working days
- Casual leave: 6 working days

Employees who join in the middle of the year receive leave on a pro-rata basis, calculated from their joining month. Public holidays (12 per year, published by HR each December) are separate and do not count against any leave balance.

## 3. Applying for Leave

All leave must be applied for through the HR portal (hr.nimbustech.example) and approved by the employee's reporting manager.

- Annual leave must be applied for at least 7 days in advance. For leave longer than 5 consecutive working days, apply at least 21 days in advance.
- Casual leave should be applied for at least 1 day in advance, except in emergencies.
- Casual leave cannot be taken for more than 2 consecutive days and cannot be combined with annual leave.

Managers should approve or reject a request within 3 working days. A request with no response after 3 working days is escalated automatically to the department head.

## 4. Sick Leave

Sick leave can be applied on the same day by informing the manager before 10:00 AM and updating the HR portal within 2 working days of returning. A medical certificate from a registered doctor is required for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be used to extend annual leave or a holiday weekend without a medical certificate.

## 5. Carry Forward and Encashment

- Up to 10 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next calendar year. Any days above 10 lapse on 31 December.
- Sick leave and casual leave cannot be carried forward; unused days lapse on 31 December.
- Annual leave cannot be encashed while employed. At resignation, up to 10 unused annual leave days are paid out at the basic daily salary rate.

## 6. Special Leave

- Maternity leave: 26 weeks of paid leave, as per the Maternity Benefit Act.
- Paternity leave: 10 working days of paid leave, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption.
- Bereavement leave: 5 working days of paid leave on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, or parent-in-law).
- Marriage leave: 5 working days of paid leave, once during employment.

## 7. Leave Without Pay

If an employee has used all their leave, additional time off is treated as Leave Without Pay (LWP). LWP needs approval from both the reporting manager and HR. More than 30 days of LWP in a year requires approval from the HR Head.
