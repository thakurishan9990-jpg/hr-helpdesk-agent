# Code of Conduct

Nimbus Technologies Pvt. Ltd. — Policy HR-03 — Effective 1 April 2026

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Code of Conduct describes the behaviour Nimbus Technologies expects from every employee, intern and contractor, at the office, while working remotely, and at company events.

## 2. Respect and Anti-Harassment

Nimbus has zero tolerance for harassment, bullying or discrimination based on gender, religion, caste, age, disability, sexual orientation or any other personal characteristic. Sexual harassment complaints are handled by the Internal Committee (IC) under the POSH Act, 2013. Complaints can be raised by email to ic@nimbustech.example, and the IC must complete its inquiry within 90 days.

## 3. Conflict of Interest

Employees must disclose any situation where personal interests may conflict with the company's interests, such as owning shares in a vendor or hiring a relative. Disclosures are made to HR using the Conflict of Interest form. Taking up a second paid job or freelance work needs written approval from HR.

## 4. Gifts and Hospitality

Employees may accept gifts from clients or vendors worth up to INR 2,000. Any gift worth more than INR 2,000 must be declared to HR within 7 days and may need to be returned. Cash or cash-equivalent gifts (such as gift cards) must never be accepted.

## 5. Confidentiality and Data Privacy

Employees must protect confidential information about the company, its clients and its employees. Personal data of colleagues (such as phone numbers, home addresses, salaries or health information) must only be accessed for valid work reasons and never shared outside the company. This duty continues after an employee leaves the company.

## 6. Use of Social Media

Employees must not share confidential information, client names or internal discussions on social media. When posting about the industry, make it clear the views are personal and not those of Nimbus Technologies.

## 7. Dress Code

Smart casual dress is the norm in the office. Business formal dress is expected for client meetings and external events. Fridays are casual dress days.

## 8. Reporting Concerns (Whistleblower Policy)

Employees can report unethical behaviour, fraud or policy violations confidentially at ethics@nimbustech.example. Reports can be anonymous. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is itself a serious violation of this Code.

## 9. Disciplinary Action

Breaking this Code can lead to a verbal warning, a written warning, suspension or termination, depending on how serious the violation is. Serious violations such as fraud, harassment or data theft can lead to immediate termination.
