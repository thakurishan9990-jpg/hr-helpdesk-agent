# IT, Laptop and Password Policy

Nimbus Technologies Pvt. Ltd. — Policy IT-01 — Effective 1 April 2026

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy protects Nimbus Technologies' devices, data and systems. It applies to every employee, intern and contractor who uses a company device or account.

## 2. Company Laptops

- Every employee is issued one company laptop on their first day. Engineering roles receive a 16 GB RAM laptop; other roles receive an 8 GB RAM laptop.
- Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. An early replacement needs a written request from the manager to IT Support.
- Only software from the Nimbus Software Center may be installed. Any other software needs IT Support approval through a ticket.
- Personal use should be minimal. Never store personal files such as photos or movies on the company laptop.
- The laptop must be returned to IT on the last working day. A missing or damaged laptop will be deducted from the final settlement at its depreciated value.

## 3. Lost or Stolen Devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to IT Support within 2 hours by calling the IT helpline or raising a P1 ticket. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device. For theft, the employee must also file a police report (FIR) and share a copy with IT within 3 days.

## 4. Password Rules

- Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a special character.
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days. The last 5 passwords cannot be reused.
- Never share your password with anyone, including IT Support. IT will never ask for your password.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) using the Nimbus Authenticator app is mandatory for email, VPN and the HR portal.

## 5. Account Lockout and Password Reset

After 5 failed login attempts, the account is locked for 30 minutes. To unlock it sooner or to reset a forgotten password, use the self-service portal at reset.nimbustech.example (you will need your MFA device) or raise a ticket with IT Support.

## 6. Raising an IT Support Ticket

Raise IT requests on the IT Service Desk portal (itsupport.nimbustech.example). Ticket priorities and target response times are:

- P1 (critical, e.g. lost device, security incident, company-wide outage): 30 minutes
- P2 (high, e.g. cannot work at all): 4 hours
- P3 (normal, e.g. software install, access request): 1 working day

## 7. Email and Data Security

Do not forward company emails to personal email accounts. Report suspicious or phishing emails using the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook. Confidential files must be stored only on the company OneDrive, never on USB drives; USB storage is blocked on all company laptops.
