# Work From Home Policy

Nimbus Technologies Pvt. Ltd. — Policy HR-02 — Effective 1 April 2026

## 1. Purpose and Scope

Nimbus Technologies follows a hybrid working model. This policy explains who can work from home (WFH), how often, and what is expected while working remotely. It applies to all employees whose role can be performed remotely. Roles that need physical presence (for example, on-site IT hardware support or facilities) are not eligible for regular WFH.

## 2. Eligibility

Employees become eligible for WFH after completing their 3-month probation period. Employees on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) may have WFH paused at their manager's discretion.

## 3. Hybrid Schedule

- Eligible employees may work from home up to 2 days per week.
- Every employee must be in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays, which are team collaboration days.
- WFH days cannot be carried forward to the next week.
- Fully remote work for a longer period (for example, for a medical reason or relocation) needs written approval from the department head and HR, and is granted for a maximum of 3 months at a time.

## 4. Requesting a WFH Day

Employees mark planned WFH days on the HR portal at least 1 working day in advance. The reporting manager may decline a WFH request when in-person presence is needed, such as client visits, workshops or team events.

## 5. Working Hours and Availability

While working from home, employees must:

- Be available on chat and email during core hours of 10:30 AM to 4:30 PM IST.
- Join all scheduled meetings with their camera on when asked.
- Respond to messages from their manager or team within 30 minutes during core hours.

## 6. Home Office Setup and Allowance

Employees are responsible for a safe and quiet workspace and a reliable internet connection of at least 50 Mbps. Nimbus provides a monthly internet allowance of INR 1,000, paid with salary, to all WFH-eligible employees. A one-time home office allowance of up to INR 5,000 is available for a chair or desk; claim it through the expense reimbursement process with an original bill.

## 7. Security While Working Remotely

All work must be done on the company-issued laptop. Employees must connect through the company VPN when accessing internal systems and must not work on confidential data over public Wi-Fi without the VPN. See the IT and Laptop Policy for full details.
