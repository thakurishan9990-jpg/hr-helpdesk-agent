# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Nimbus Technologies Pvt. Ltd. — Policy FIN-01 — Effective 1 April 2026

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy explains which business expenses Nimbus Technologies reimburses and how to claim them. It applies to all employees who spend their own money for approved company work.

## 2. How to Submit a Claim

- Submit claims on the Expense portal within 30 days of spending the money. Claims older than 30 days are rejected unless the department head approves the delay.
- Every claim needs an original bill or invoice. A bill with GST details is required for any expense above INR 2,000.
- Claims are approved by the reporting manager and then checked by the Finance team.

## 3. Reimbursement Timeline

Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary if approved by the 20th of the month. Claims approved after the 20th are paid with the following month's salary.

## 4. Travel Expenses

- Domestic flights: economy class only, booked at least 7 days in advance through the company travel desk.
- Trains: AC 2-tier or AC chair car.
- Local travel: cab or auto fares are reimbursed at actuals for client visits; daily office commute is not reimbursed.
- Hotels: up to INR 4,000 per night in metro cities and INR 3,000 per night in other cities.

## 5. Meals and Daily Allowance

During official outstation travel, employees receive a daily meal allowance of INR 1,200 in metro cities and INR 800 in other cities. No bills are needed for the daily allowance. Team lunches or client meals need prior approval from the manager and are reimbursed up to INR 1,500 per person.

## 6. Other Reimbursable Expenses

- Work-related certification exams: reimbursed up to INR 25,000 per year after passing, with manager approval before registering.
- Home office allowance: up to INR 5,000, once (see the Work From Home Policy).
- Mobile phone bill: up to INR 750 per month for roles marked "on-call" by the department head.

## 7. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are never reimbursed: alcohol, traffic fines, personal entertainment, upgrades to business class without approval, and expenses without a valid bill (other than the daily allowance).

## 8. Misuse

Submitting fake bills or claiming the same expense twice is treated as misconduct under the Code of Conduct and may lead to disciplinary action, including termination.
